Event Rules

I. Event Overview

Apply monthly to unlock an exclusive Room Tag and Room Frame upon approval. Complete monthly tasks to earn up to $30.

II. Event Schedule

Launch Date: Starts Sept 1, 2026 (UTC+8).

Registration: 1st 00:00 – 10th 23:59 monthly (UTC+8).

Event & Settlement: 1st 00:00 – Last day 23:59 monthly (UTC+8).

III. Tasks

Task 1 | Live Streaming

Reach 20 valid streaming days. A valid live day requires ≥ 2 hours of effective streaming time.

Task 2 | Invitation

Invite ≥ 5 new users via your code/link who spend ≥ 10,000 coins before settlement.

Task 3 | Promotion

Upload 4 screenshots of your promotional posts about the App from external platforms.

IV. Rewards & Settlement

Decorations (Tag & Frame)

Issued upon approval, valid until the month's end. Re-apply next month to renew.

Cash Rewards (Paid to Agency Account)

Max Reward ($30): Complete Task 1 + Task 2 + Task 3.

Base Reward ($21): Complete Task 1 only.

No Reward ($0): Fail Task 1 (Task 1 is mandatory).

V. Q&A

Q: Will data prior to my approval date count?

A: Yes, system automatically backtracks data from the 1st of the month.

Q: Do decorations expire?

A: Yes, valid until the last day of the month. Re-apply next month for new ones.

Q: Do I need to re-apply every month?

A: Yes, each calendar month is settled independently.
