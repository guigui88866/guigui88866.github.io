Event Rules

I. Event Overview

Apply monthly to unlock an exclusive Room Tag and Room Frame upon approval. Complete monthly tasks to earn up to $30.

II. Event Schedule

Launch Date: Starts Sept 30, 2026 (UTC+8).

Registration Period: 1st 00:00 – 10th 23:59 monthly (UTC+8).

Event & Settlement Period: 1st 00:00 – Last day 23:59 monthly (UTC+8).

III. Task Requirements

Task 1 | Live Streaming (Mandatory)

Reach ≥ 20 valid streaming days (≥ 2 hrs/day).

Task 2 | Invitation

Invite ≥ 5 new valid users via your exclusive code/link who spend ≥ 14,000 coins before monthly settlement.

Task 3 | Promotion

Upload 4 valid screenshots of your promotional posts about the App on external social media platforms (Subject to admin approval).

IV. Rewards & Settlement Rules

1. Room Decorations (Tag & Frame)

Issued immediately upon registration approval.

Valid until 23:59:59 (UTC+8) on the last day of the current month. Re-apply next month to renew.

2. Cash Rewards (Paid to Agency Account)

Mandatory Threshold: Task 1 is required for ALL payouts. If you fail to complete Task 1 (< 20 valid days), your total reward will be $0, and all other task bonuses will be forfeited.

Payout Structure (Upon completing Task 1):

Task 1 Only: $20

Task 1 + Task 3 (Promotion): $22

Task 1 + Task 2 (Invitation): $25

Full Mastery (All Tasks Completed): $30 (Includes an extra $3 Full Mastery Bonus)

V. Frequently Asked Questions (Q&A)

Q: Will streaming and task data prior to my approval date count?

A: Yes. Once approved, the system automatically backtracks and calculates all your task data from the 1st 00:00:00 (UTC+8) of the current month.

Q: What happens if I don't reach 20 valid streaming days?

A: Task 1 is a mandatory requirement. If you stream less than 20 valid days, your final payout for the month will be $0, even if you completed the Invitation or Promotion tasks.

Q: Do room decorations expire?

A: Yes. Decorations are valid until the last day of the month. You need to re-apply in the next calendar month to get new decorations.

Q: Do I need to re-apply every month?

A: Yes. Each calendar month operates independently. You must apply between the 1st and 10th of each month to participate.
